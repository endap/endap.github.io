It is an internal meeting of a program 26d21 continuing previous ux 26h35 , where the focus of the sub-team is MFL sensor technology.

Magnetic flux leakage (MFL) is a non-destructive testing (NDT) technique that has numerous applications across a wide range of industries 1, such in structural reinforcement detection 2, safety evaluation of engineering struckture 3, ferromagnetic plate inspections in metal industry 4, and has been successfully used for nearly 60 years to detect, identify, and size metal loss due to corrosion 5. Its main principles, measurement, data processing 6, recent advances 7, and advantages-diadvantages information 8 are available. Several approaches are used to study the signal, e.g. Zatsepin-Shcherbinin and Edwards-Palmer models 9, multiple data matching methods 10, convolutional neural network (CNN) model 11, and forming mechanism of hidden signal 12.
