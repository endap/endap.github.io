Learning to run Workbench 2026 R1 again.

open-save as

  1. Press Window key ⊞ and type workbench and press enter.
  2. Find in the menu bar File ⟶ Save As until the Open/Save Dialog Box opened.
  3. Navigate to target working folder.
  4. Provide filename e.g. v500x2000.wbpj, a Workbench Project File and click Save.

import geometry

  1. Find in the Toolbox on the left Analysis Systems.
  2. Click + to expand it.
  3. Scroll down and find Static Structural.
  4. Drag it to the Project Schematic area.
  5. Right click on Geometry ⟶ Import Geometry ⟶ Browse.
  6. Navigate and choose the .iges file to import, e.g. 3d-fender-v-500x2000.iges.
  7. Click Open.

fix gemoetry

  1. Skip this part for now.

material

  1. Double click Engineering Data.
  2. In menu bar find View &xarr; Reset Window Layout, if necessary or some windows are closed accidentally.
  3. Find Outline of Schematic A2: Engineering Data.
  4. Find Click here to add new material (usually below Sturctural Steel).
  5. Click it and type Rubber.
  6. Look on the left and the Toolbox should become active.
  7. Find and expand Hyperelastic.
  8. Find Neo-Hookean and double click it.
  9. Notice that there is new row in Properties of Outline Row 4: Rubber.
  10. Fill value of 1e+6 for Intial Shear Modulus Mu, then press enter, and keep the unit in Pa.
  11. Fill value of 2e-0 for Incompressibility Parameter D1, then press enter, and keep the unit in Pa^-1.
  12. Notice that previous two cells with yellow background are now having white background.

model

  1. Back to Project tab and find Project Schematic.
  2. Double click Model.
  3. Wait until A: Static Structural - Mechanical [Ansys Mechanical Enterprise] is opened.
  4. Find Outline tree on the left.
  5. Find Geometry and expand it.
  6. Click Body, e.g. 3d-fender-v-500x2000, and notice that the model become green, where it was previously gray.
  7. Find on lower left window Material.
  8. If there is not any Rubber, then close Mechanical.
  9. Back to Workbench Project Schematic.

material

  1. Double click Engineering Data.
  2. Confirm that Rubber exist.
  3. Close Engineering Data.
  4. Right click on Engineering Data &xarr; Update.

model

  1. Double click Model in Static Structural.
  2. Expand Geometry on Outline tree.
  3. Choose the Body, e.g. 3d-fender-v-500x2000.
  4. Find Material on the lower left.
  5. Click right part of Assignment and choose Rubber.
  6. Find Mesh on Outline tree.
  7. Right click and choose Generate Mesh.

analysis

  1. Find Static Structural on the Outline tree.
  2. Click it and go to the lower left window.
  3. Find Solver Control and expand it.
  4. Set Large Deflection On.

support

  1. Find Static Structural on the Outline tree.
  2. Right click Insert &xarr; Fixed Support.
  3. Rotate model and until bottom plate visible.
  4. Left click on it and choose Apply Selection (double arrow left and righ).

load

  1. Find Static Structural on the Outline tree.
  2. Right click Insert &xarr; Displacement.
  3. Rotate model and until top part visible, where to apply displacement.
  4. Change Z Component to Constant.
  5. Fill in the table -1e-2

solve

  1. Find Solution on the Outline tree.
  2. Right click Solve.
  3. Wait the process.

reaction force

  1. Find Solution in Outline tree.
  2. Right click Insert &xarr; Proble &xarr; Force Reaction.
  3. Find on lower left Definition and expand it.
  4. Change Location Method to Geometry Selection.
  5. Click on a face and choose Apply Selection.
  6. Back to Analysys Settings and find Output Controls.
  7. Set Nodal Forces to Yes.

solve

  1. Find Solution on the Outline tree.
  2. Right click Clear Generated Data.
  3. Right click Solve.
  4. Wait the process.

modification

  1. Change displacement from -0.01 to -0.l m.
  2. Repeat the required steps.
  3. Change Scoping Method to Geometri Selection.
  4. Click in the Model the face and choose Apply Selection.
  5. Set Orientation to Z.

displacement

  1. Right on Solution.
  2. Insert Deformation &xarr; Directional Deformation.

force - displacement chart

  1. Find Solution in menu bar.
  2. In solutin find Chart.
  3. Fill X-Axis with Deformation.
  4. Fill Y-Axis with Reaction Force.
  5. In Chart Controls X Axis to Directional Deformation (Min).

  1. Change Z Component of Displacement to 0.225 m (45%).
  2. Analysis Setting &xarr; Step Controls.
  3. Initiat Substeps 10, Min 10, Max 100.
