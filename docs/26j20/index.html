text summary 1

  • Summerizer use background knowledge, experience, and even feeling to what really matter to him/her.
  • Summerizing text does not only make the text shorter.
  • It is a non-linear process jumping back and forth.
  • It is involving information verification and evaluation, and thoughts organization.
  • In simply way, it is a complex and messy process.

ai text summarizer tools 2.

  • They allow researchers to outsource their initial article summerization to AI.
  • Most AI summarization tools fall into 3 categories
    • summary generator,
    • AI PDF chatbot,
    • a suit of tools of integrated AI summerizer.
  • Logically, SciSpace, Resoomer, ChatPDF, ChatGPT, Scholarcy, TLDR This, and Quillbot Summerizer are some examples.

reading techniques 3

Technique Meaning Speed Purpose
Skimming Quick look for main idea Fast To know what the text is about
Scanning Searching for one detail Very Fast To find specific information
Close Reading Slow and detailed reading Slow To understand deeply

skimming strategy 4

  • Read the first and last paragraphs of an article first.
  • Notice the titles, headings, and subheadings.
  • Look at the illustrations, graphs, or other visuals on the page.
  • Read the captions of the visuals.
  • Read the first sentence of each paragraph.

ai’s impact on reading 5

  • AI-reading assistant that can summarize anything into any language can have immense benefit for second-language learners.
  • It also plays an important role in equity and access to information.
  • Uncritical adoption of AI reading tools poses an incredible risk to student’s close reading skills.

misinformation 1

  • Misinformation, bullshit, and overgeneralizations are inherent limitations of LLM-based chatbot.
  • It is so-called hallucination and still pose a huge problem.
  • This misinformation is not a technical error or the delusion of an artificial “brain”.
  • It is the result of GenAI’s baked-in indifference to the truth probability.

reading benefit 6

  • Creativity and originality are important traits, where reading is a keyway to obtain these.
  • Reading can also give other benefits such as critical thinking skills as well as just overall brain-strengthening with vocabulary and comprehension.
  • A major downside of having AI do reading for someone is that “the reading assistances [are] focused on speed and task completion over nurturing developing skills".
  • The right answer can be still obtained using AI in summarizing a reading, but the work and benefits of reading the given text oneself is completely taken away.

mermaid 7

  • It is a diagram described entirely in text.
  • It can be used to describe almost any kind of diagram: flowcharts, sequence diagrams, class diagrams, entity-relationship diagrams, and more.
  • Engineers have used Mermaid for years to keep diagrams in their codebase, right next to the code they describe.
  • Current large language models are very good at writing it.
  • Ask an AI coding agent to diagram your system, and there is a good chance it hands you back Mermaid syntax, because it is the format that it has learned to write well.

mermaid summary benefit 8

  • Compared with paragraphs/itemized summaries: it makes relationships, hierarchy, and cause–effect links explicit, not just the key points.
  • Compared with graphical concept maps: it remains text-based, searchable, editable, copyable, version-controllable, and easy for AI to generate or modify.
  • Compared with both: Mermaid can be rendered into a visual map when needed, while retaining the underlying structured text.
  • It is also easier to reuse in documents, websites, notes, or further AI processing than a static image.
