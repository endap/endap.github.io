abstract

The 2026 International Statistics Seminar
Applications of Statistical Learning and Machine Learning in Multidisciplinary Research

Keynote Title: Conversational Physics: LLM-Guided Simulation for Non-Programmers

Abstract (max: 300 words): Large Language Models (LLMs) are transforming how computational models and interactive learning environments can be developed, potentially lowering the programming barrier for users who want to explore complex physical and statistical phenomena. This work presents a research trajectory toward Conversational Physics, an approach in which natural-language interaction with LLMs supports the construction, refinement, and exploration of simulation-based models by non-programmers.

The talk brings together three complementary studies. The first examined the development of a Doppler-effect simulation through iterative prompt engineering, focusing on physics accuracy, sound-wave visualisation, and user interface and experience. The second extended the approach to electric-field simulation, introducing greater challenges in vector-based physics, numerical stability, dynamic visualisation, and interaction. Across both studies, iterative prompting enabled the development of functional and conceptually accurate web-based simulations, while expert verification remained essential for identifying implementation limitations and ensuring educational quality.

The third study demonstrates how computational physics can be integrated with statistical modelling to investigate one-on-one futsal goal-scoring probability. Using agent-based kinematics and shooting geometry, the model estimates Expected Goals (xG) by combining goal-angle projection with goalkeeper spatial occupancy. Variations in projectile velocity, reaction latency, and wingspan were analysed using simulation and sigmoid regression, revealing a critical response-time threshold of approximately 385 ms and systematic changes in goal-scoring probability.

Together, these studies illustrate a continuum from natural-language-driven simulation development to physics-based statistical inference. The keynote highlights the potential of LLMs to democratise computational modelling while emphasising the continuing role of human expertise in physical validation, statistical interpretation, numerical reliability, and research design. The broader significance lies in establishing conversational interfaces as a bridge between domain knowledge, simulation, and statistical analysis for researchers, educators, and non-programmers.

Keywords: Large Language Models (LLMs); Conversational Physics; Simulation; Statistical Modelling; Expected Goals (xG).

  • Pemodelan Peluang Gol pada Futsal Satu Lawan Satu Menggunakan Simulasi Kinematika dan Geometri Shooting 1.
    • Penelitian ini mengkaji dinamika interaksi antara penyerang dan penjaga gawang dalam olahraga futsal melalui pemodelan kinematika berbasis agen. Fokus utama penelitian ini adalah menguantifikasi pengaruh limitasi biologis berupa waktu reaksi motorik penjaga gawang dan jangkauan lengan (wingspan) terhadap peluang gol yang dinyatakan dalam nilai Expected Goals (xG). Simulasi dibangun menggunakan parameter atlet elit dengan variasi kelajuan proyektil (15, 25, dan 35 m/s) dan ambang batas latensi motorik sebesar 200 ms. Penentuan nilai xG dilakukan dengan mengintegrasikan proyeksi sudut gawang (goal angle) dan luas okupansi ruang penjaga gawang menggunakan algoritma bisektor. Analisis regresi sigmoid menunjukkan adanya pergeseran titik kritis peluang (𝑑0) yang signifikan, dari 5,78 m pada kelajuan rendah hingga 13,34 m pada kelajuan ekstrem. Temuan ini mengonfirmasi adanya konstanta waktu kritis sistem sebesar ±385 ms sebagai ambang batas kemampuan penyelamatan aktif. Hasil penelitian menyimpulkan bahwa peningkatan energi kinetik proyektil secara fisis mampu mengompensasi keterbatasan sudut geometri, yang secara efektif mereduksi efektivitas pertahanan penjaga gawang melalui mekanisme reflex bottleneck.
  • Development of ChatGPT-Based Physics Simulations: From the Doppler Effect to Electric Field Exploration 2:
    • The emergence of Large Language Models (LLMs) such as ChatGPT provides new opportunities to support the development of interactive physics simulations through iterative prompt engineering. This study evaluates the robustness of this approach by extending its application from a Doppler Effect simulation to an electric field simulation, which involves greater complexity in physics implementation, dynamic visualization, and user interaction. The simulation was developed iteratively using ChatGPT as a coding assistant and evaluated through self-evaluation and expert evaluation covering physics concept, visualisation, and UI/UX aspects. The results indicate that the iterative prompt engineering approach successfully produced an accurate and interactive web-based simulation despite the increased development complexity. Nevertheless, implementing vector-based physics, ensuring numerical stability, and refining dynamic field-line visualisation still required iterative debugging and expert verification. These findings suggest that while ChatGPT can substantially accelerate physics simulation development, human expertise remains essential to validate physics accuracy, improve educational usability, and ensure the overall quality of the resulting learning media.
  • From prompt to physics: evaluating Doppler effect simulation generated by ChatGPT 3.
    • This study aims to develop and evaluate a Doppler effect simulation generated by ChatGPT. The development process was carried out through iterative prompt engineering, focusing on user interface, user experience, sound wave visualisation, and the accuracy of physics concept representation. The simulation was assessed through self-evaluation and expert evaluation, using a Likert scale and qualitative analysis. The results showed that the simulation accurately represents the underlying physics concepts, achieving maximum scores in both conceptual and visualisation aspects. Nevertheless, some limitations were identified regarding interface flexibility. This study highlights ChatGPT’s considerable potential as a tool for developing physics simulations, although ongoing evaluation is necessary for educational appropriateness.
  • Pemodelan Peluang Gol pada Futsal Satu-Lawan-Satu Menggunakan Simulasi Kinematika dan Geometri Shooting 4:
    • Penelitian ini memperluas metrik Expected Goals (xG) pada skenario futsal satu- lawan-satu menjadi fungsi probabilitas gabungan spasio-temporal menggunakan Agent-Based Modeling. Melalui metode Monte Carlo berbasis komputasi JavaScript, probabilitas gol dievaluasi dengan mengonfrontasikan kecepatan proyektil (15, 25, dan 35 m/s) terhadap latensi motorik biologis penjaga gawang (200 ms). Perhitungan ruang lintasan diekstraksi secara dinamis menggunakan algoritma bisektor sudut bukaan gawang (open angle) dan terintegrasi dengan Continuous Collision Detection (CCD). Hasil regresi fungsi Sigmoid terhadap data simulasi membuktikan bahwa peningkatan energi kinetik memicu pergeseran drastis pada titik ekuilibrium (peluang gol 50%). Jarak kritis (x0) terdorong mundur dari 9,23 meter pada kecepatan 15 m/s menjadi 19,24 meter pada 35 m/s, sementara ambang batas sudut ( ?0 ) menyusut ketat dari 0,21 menjadi 0,09 radian. Disimpulkan bahwa momentum proyektil ekstrem memicu kelumpuhan reaksi (reaction paralysis) yang secara efektif menetralkan parameter jarak tembak. Peluang gol terbukti merupakan hasil irisan mutlak antara ketersediaan celah geometri dan keunggulan waktu kinematika, di mana sisa probabilitas kegagalan asimtotik murni diakibatkan oleh inakurasi biomekanik penendang (angular noise).
