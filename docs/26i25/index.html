marine-fender fea-simulation

  • Sparisoma Viridi
11 Sep 2026 · 3 mins read · edit

In order to protect both vessels and quay structures from damage by absorbing kinetic energy during berthing, marine or ship fenders are used 1. There are various type of fenders, e.g. multi-layer one 2, where fea simulation is a common way to study the performance of fenders during impact 3. The nearest structer similar to fender is bumper 4, where for car it can be simulated using Ansys Mechanical 5.

preparations

  • Intial learning will be using simple structure, Neo-Hookean hyperelastic material model, fixed constaint at one fasce and compress/displace at opposite face, fine mesh and high-order solid elements, large deformation, and analysis of force-displacement curve, deformation, equivalent/maximum principal strain, stress distribution, also comparae with simple theoretical calculation or experimental data 6.
  • There is proposed workflow to use experimental data in FEA simulation 7.
  • Ansys Student can be used as long it is for student assignment and self-learning 8.
  • Currently there are Ansys Student, Ansys LS-DYNA Student, Ansys Electronics Desktop Student, Ansys Scade One Student, and Ansys Rocky Student Add-Ins available 9.
