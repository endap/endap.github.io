The available about 40 samples can be modeled starting with composition-dependent linear or quadratic regression of Young’s modulus 1, which is better to use a two-level modeling aprroach 2. Demonstrating a relationship between compositional disorder, microstructural configurational entropy, and fluctuations in macroscopic mechanical properties might have potentially publishable novelty 3.
