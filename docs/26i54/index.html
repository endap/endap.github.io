It is an attempt to finalize the report with conclusion and create deliverable. For a multdisciplinary project divisions do not necessarily need to reach the same conclusion or recommend the same design / direction / scheme, but each division evaluates the product from its own perspective 1. The project deliverable 2 should usually not jump directly to one design/recommendation but it can describe a strategic trade-off, not simply a difference in product design or recommendation 3.

notes

  • There might be some related notes, e.g. 26i02 , 26i06 , 26i14 and others.
  • Try to overcome the barrier and get a good advice as a principle: 4
    “Progress first, quality second, organization third.”
  • There is cycle, i.e. provisional conlusion cycle, suggested to use for this kind of problem 5.
  • In other project the unreadiness is felt very strong, while lighter in this one and all due to the trust of the team 6.
  • There are at least three different layers of MFL as mental model 7.
  • Previous factors are compated into a table as a taxonomy of MFL technology 8.
