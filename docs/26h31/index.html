plot

Previous datasets can be plotted in the same chart as follows.

Figure 1. Probability of sum of $n$ dice for $n$ = 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.

Values from previous section are normalized to the sum of all possible combination of $n$ dice. Results shown in Figure 1 are as expected, where $s$ and $p$ stand for sum of dice and probability of sum, respectively. It also confirms the formulation 3

$$\tag{1} P(Y = Y|n) = \frac{1}{6^n} \sum^M_{i=0} (-1)^i \binom{n}{i} \binom{y-6i-1}{n-1}, $$

where

$$\tag{2} M = \left\lfloor \frac{y-n}{6} \right\rfloor $$

is the greates integer that less than or equal to $(y-n)/6$.
