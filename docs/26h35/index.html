In the detection and inspection of potential hazards within the pipeline network in-line inspection (ILI) techniques have been widely used 1. Sensor-equipped robotic devices integrated with intelligent algorithms to perform internal pipeline assessments, enabling non-contact, high-precision defect monitoring are all utilized in these technologies 2. Inline inspection involves inserting intelligent pigs—advanced, sensor-equipped devices—into the pipeline to collect real-time data during flow, as its core, that can assess wall thickness, detect corrosion, and identify cracks or deformities that may compromise the structural integrity of the pipeline 3.
