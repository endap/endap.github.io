Some religions offer its people opportunity to conduct a pilgrimage 1, which would be better to be financed via a scheme 2, that must be assured not to be a variant of Ponzi scheme 3, when the fund is invested to give fiancial benefit to the participants.

There is an interesting point of view related to pilgrimage, which can be considered as a form of ecotherapy 4, an emerging field whose current consensus among experts saying that spending time outdoors can have a positive impact on your mental health 5.
