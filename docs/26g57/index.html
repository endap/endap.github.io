There is an interesting information about how the MQ-x gas sensors work in a meeting 1. The heating creates an active surface, electrons removal from the surface by oxygen increases resistance, and reducing gas that return electros decreases resistance 2. Deeper understanding can be obtained by intruducing the concept of depletion layer in semiconductor 3. What I am missing in the beginning is the fact that the sensing film is porous so the pores allow gas to diffuse throughout the film 4.
