notes

  • It is stored in 10 of qb2-misc-topics 26d01 .
  • For data and AI literacy there are some suggested topics 1, where it is a little bit difference from digital and AI literacy 2, but rather similar when only about AI literacy 3.
  • There is an IA framework proposed by Stanford University as follows 4
    • Functional literacy: How does AI work?
    • Ethical literacy: How do we navigate the ethical issues of AI?
    • Rhetorical literacy: How do we use natural and AI-generated language to achieve our goals?
    • Pedagogical literacy: How do we use AI to enhance teaching and learning?
  • Another one is AILit Framework that covers competences and learner expectations such as 5
    • Engage with AI,
    • Create with AI,
    • Manage AI,
    • Shape AI.
