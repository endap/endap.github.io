calorimetry

  • Sparisoma Viridi
19 Jun 2026 · 2 mins read · edit

The process of measuring the amount of heat released or absorbed during a chemical reaction is known as calorimetry 1, which uses the law conservation energy 2 and specific heat capacity 3, where the common cases are simple hot water loop 4, usage of cooktops for water heating 5, dry roast 6, and electrified nickel wire 7. The last three require also other equations.

equation

When two substances at different temperatures come in contact, the heat transfer is calculated using 8

$$\tag{1} Q = m c \Delta T, $$

where $Q$ is the heat transferred, $m$ is mass of the substance, $c$ is substance specific heat capacity, and $\Delta T$ is the change in temperature.
