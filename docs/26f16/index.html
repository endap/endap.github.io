quadratic formula

  • Sparisoma Viridi
9 Jun 2026 · 1 min read · edit

Quadratic formula gives the roots of a quadratic equation 1, which is a closed-form expression describing the solutions 2. In finding the solution, while factoring will not always work, the quadratic formula can always give the answer 3. There are explanation about the formula derivation 4 and online calculator for the formula 5.

equation

A quadratic equation

$$ \tag{1} ax^2 + bx + c = 0 $$

will have roots $x_1$ and $x_2$ which is function of $a$, $b$, and $c$.

formula

Closed-form expression of the solution is

$$ \tag{2} x_{1,2} = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}. $$

Order of $x_1$ and $x_2$ still depends on sign of $a$ 6 7.
