This post is a typical note about something that I know just a little, use LLM to get the term, and then find the other references based on the term. This time it is about compound interest model 1, which is also title of this post.

Compound interest can be considered as interest on interest, which refers to the total amount of interest calculated, not only on initial investment or loan but also on the accumulated interest obtained from previous years and can be calculated annually, quarterly, or monthly 2.

The interest rate, how often interest is added, and how long the money stays in the account are three factors determining how much compound interest adds to an account over time 3, where it is constrasted with simple interest 4. There is available online calculator for calculating it 5.
